Last updated: 15 November 2026

Summary: FocusLock does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app needs stays on your phone.

Data we collect

None. FocusLock has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no internet permission. It cannot send any information off your device.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves a few settings locally on your phone only:

  • the apps you chose to block,
  • the length and end time of your current lock session,
  • your unlock task setting and progress (for example, how many math questions you solved).

This information never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Accessibility service

FocusLock uses Android's Accessibility service (AccessibilityService API) for one purpose: to detect which app is opened, so it can show the lock screen over apps you chose to block during a focus session that you started yourself. During a lock, it also covers Settings, app stores and the uninstall screen so the lock cannot be bypassed. Calls and emergency calls always keep working.

The service does not read the content of your screen, your messages, what you type or your passwords, and it does not collect or share any data. You are asked for permission with a clear explanation before the service is enabled, and you can turn it off at any time when no lock is running.

Installed apps list

FocusLock reads the list of apps installed on your phone only to show it to you, so you can choose which ones to block. This list is not stored elsewhere or shared.

Children

FocusLock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
